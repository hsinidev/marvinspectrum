### Game Details & Guide
Marvin's Spectrum





Game Overview



Marvin's Spectrum is a browser-based puzzle game where players aim to create different colors by combining adjacent squares of the same color. The objective is to clear all the remaining squares on the board, scoring points for each cleared square.


How to Play:

• Click and drag squares to move them around the board.
• Each adjacent square must be of the same color.
• Clearing a row or column will eliminate that many colored squares from the board.
• Your goal is to clear all the remaining squares on the board.



Controls:


• Use your mouse to click and drag squares around the board.
• Right-click to reveal options, but be careful not to over-extend yourself by right-clicking too far away from a square you're trying to move. You can adjust the sensitivity of your clicks to suit your playstyle.



Objective:


• Your goal is to create different colors on the board by clearing adjacent squares.
• Each cleared row or column will increase your score.



Tips and Tricks for Success:


• Keep a sharp eye out for opportunities to create two-colored rows or columns, which can help you clear multiple squares at once.
• Try to block other players' moves by placing colored squares in strategic positions.
• Clearing all the remaining squares on the board will earn you a high score.



Why You Should Play It:

• With each new level, the difficulty increases, offering a continuous challenge for experienced players.
